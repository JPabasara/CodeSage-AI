# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in CodeSage AI to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that keep this community open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being kind and respectful to other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and
  learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the community as a whole.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission;
- any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, such as the repository, its
issues and pull requests, and any discussion about the project. It also applies
when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainers through
GitHub, for example with a private report on the repository. Every report will
be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy
will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. Depending on how serious
the behaviour is, the response can be a private warning, a temporary ban, or a
permanent ban from the project.
